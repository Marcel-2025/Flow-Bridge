2. In short

  • No account, no ads, no tracking, no analytics services.
  • Input and clipboard text travel only encrypted and directly to devices you paired yourself, inside your local network.
  • We run no server for this and do not receive that data.

3. Android app FlowBridge

What the app transmits: touchpad movement, clicks, keyboard input, text you send, snippets and clipboard text. The only recipient is the computer you paired with a 6-digit code. The connection stays on the local network and is end-to-end encrypted (Noise protocol). This content is not sent to us or to third parties and is not stored by us.

Network search: when you tap “Search”, the app sends a request into your local network. FlowBridge computers answer with device name, operating system, port and a key fingerprint.

What is stored locally on your device: settings, device name, the last used computer address, your snippets, a cryptographic device key and the list of paired computers. The clipboard history is kept in memory only and is gone when the app closes. App data is excluded from Android cloud backup. You delete all data by clearing the app data in the Android settings or uninstalling the app.

Picture of the computer (from version 1.2.0): when you switch to “Screen” and the computer allows it, the app receives the picture of your computer. It travels over the same encrypted connection, is only displayed and is neither stored on the device nor sent to us or third parties.

Clipboard: the app reads your device clipboard only when you tap “send”. It writes to it only when you copy a received text or switch on automatic copying.

Permissions: internet and network state (for the connection on the local network), vibration (haptic feedback). No access to contacts, location, camera, microphone or files.

Purchases: the optional one-time purchase “FlowBridge Android Pro” is handled entirely by Google Play. The app only learns from Google whether the purchase exists. We receive no payment data. Google’s privacy policy applies.

Legal basis: where personal data is processed at all, this is done to provide the function you asked for (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

4. Desktop app FlowBridge Pro

The desktop app likewise transmits mouse and keyboard input and clipboard text only encrypted between your paired devices on the local network. From version 0.4.0 it can also send the picture of your screen to a paired phone or tablet. This is off until you switch it on; while it is being sent, the app shows a notice. The picture is not stored. Stored locally: settings, device keys, the list of paired devices and a diagnostic log of connection events without input or content.

The app contains a licence and update check that is currently inactive because no server is operated for it. Before it is activated we will extend this policy with the data processed then.
